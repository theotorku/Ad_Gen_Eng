# ProPlan Solutions — Creative Services Agreement

This Agreement is entered into by and between **ProPlan Solutions** ("Provider") and the Client named below ("Client") for the provision of monthly productized creative services.

---

### Section 1: Scope of Services, Ownership, and Boundaries

**Provider will deliver** one (1) "Campaign-in-a-Box" ad bundle every thirty (30) days, which includes ten (10) custom, brand-aligned ad image assets, multi-channel ad copy variants (headlines, primary text, and calls-to-action), and one (1) high-converting landing-page HTML/copy snippet. Upon receipt of full monthly payment, **Client holds complete ownership and copyrights** of the final delivered visual and written creative assets for use across their digital marketing channels. **Client explicitly acknowledges** that Provider is a creative asset provider only; Provider does not manage Client’s Google, Meta, or local directory ad accounts, does not handle ad budgets, and does not provide or guarantee specific cost-per-lead (CPL), call volumes, or conversion results. Client is solely responsible for compliance with local regulations, industry licensing, and ad platform terms of service.

### Section 2: Subscription, Billing, and Cancellation Terms

**Services are provided on a recurring, month-to-month subscription basis** at the rate of **$497.00 per month** (or the selected tier rate), billed automatically every thirty (30) days using the Client's credit card on file via Stripe. There are no long-term contracts, setup fees, or retainers. **Client may cancel their subscription at any time** by providing written notice via email to billing@proplansolutions.com at least **five (5) calendar days prior** to their next scheduled renewal date. Cancellation stops all future automatic billing; however, no partial-month refunds or credits will be issued, and outstanding deliverables for the current billing cycle will be completed and delivered.
