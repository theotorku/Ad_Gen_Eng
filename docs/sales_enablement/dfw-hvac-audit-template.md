## 2. The Three Creative Sins of Local HVAC Ads
*Reviewing active ads in your market shows that most businesses suffer from these three critical visual bottlenecks:*

*   **Sin #1: Commodity Stock Photos.** Using smiling actors holding clipboards or generic outdoor AC units. DFW homeowners don't trust stock models—they trust real, competent local tradespeople.
*   **Sin #2: "Me-Too" Messaging.** Headline hooks like *"Call us for AC repair"* or *"Voted #1 in DFW"* are ignored. Ads must speak directly to specific suburbs (e.g., Frisco, Plano, Arlington) and offer concrete, review-backed trust.
*   **Sin #3: Flat Visual Presence.** Logos that are tiny, low-contrast, or completely missing from the ad image. If a homeowner scrolls past your ad, you've paid for a impression where they didn't even recognize your brand.
