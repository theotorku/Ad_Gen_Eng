# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Purpose

This document is the operating GTM plan for the Ad Generation Engine. It is grounded in the product as it exists today (campaign brief intake → strategy → channel variants → image prompts → review workspace) and a live cycle-1 outreach playbook of 20 DFW-area HVAC contractors. It is written to be executed against, not pitched. Update it as offers, pricing, and conversion data change.

## Product vs. Go-to-Market — Read This First

**The Ad Generation Engine is general-purpose.** It accepts any campaign brief — any brand, product, audience, tone, or channel mix (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Search) — and produces a structured ad bundle: strategy, channel-specific copy variants, image prompts, and rendered images. Nothing in the engine is HVAC-specific.

**HVAC is a GTM choice, not a product scope.** Selling a horizontal "AI ad generator" to everyone is how we lose to Canva, AdCreative.ai, and Meta's own tooling. Selling a *vertically specialized done-for-you offer* to a buyer with weak creative, real ad spend, and an answerable phone is how we win. HVAC SMBs in DFW are the chosen beachhead. New verticals are opened by building a new prospect playbook and offer wrap — not by changing the engine.

This GTM plan therefore operates on two layers throughout:
- **Engine-level:** vertical-agnostic, applies to any future vertical we open
- **Vertical-level:** HVAC-specific in cycle 1, swapped or duplicated when we open vertical #2

## One-Line Positioning

**Engine-level:** Campaign brief in, ready-to-run ads out — strategy, copy, and rendered images for any channel and any vertical, in minutes instead of hours.

**Cycle-1 vertical (HVAC):** A done-for-you campaign-in-a-box for local HVAC contractors — branded ad images, copy, and landing snippets delivered monthly, priced between DIY tools and full-service agencies.

## What We Are Not

- Not a self-serve SaaS for end-customers (yet). The engine is general-purpose, but the current go-to-market shape is operator-run; pretending otherwise creates support load we cannot absorb.
- Not a horizontal "AI ad generator" sold to everyone. The engine *can* serve any vertical, but the GTM is verticalized on purpose. That lane (horizontal commodity AI tools) is crowded by Canva, AdCreative.ai, Pencil, and Meta's own tooling — we do not win there.
- Not an agency. We do not bid on retainers, we do not manage ad spend, we do not promise CPL.

## Beachhead Market (Cycle 1)

**Primary:** independent HVAC contractors in the Dallas–Fort Worth metro doing $2M–$15M/yr revenue, owner-operated, currently running Google LSAs / Yelp / Facebook ads with stale or commodity creative.

**Why HVAC was chosen as the beachhead** (the engine works for any vertical — these are the criteria that made HVAC win the cycle-1 slot):
- Hot seasonal demand (Texas summer) creates urgency every May–September.
- Owners answer their own phones — outbound actually works.
- Creative is visibly weak across the category; differentiation is cheap.
- The 20-prospect outreach playbook already exists and is researched.

**General criteria for picking a beachhead vertical** (apply when evaluating vertical #2, #3, etc.):
- Active ad spend in the $1.5K–$15K/mo range
- Owner-operator decision-maker reachable by phone
- Visibly weak or commodity creative across the category
- A natural seasonal or event-driven urgency window
- Realistic prospect density of 200+ shops in a single metro

**Vertical expansion queue** (in unlock order, each opens by building a new prospect playbook + offer wrap on the same engine):
1. Roofing — storm-driven seasonality, similar buyer profile, larger ticket.
2. Plumbing — overlaps with HVAC on emergency-call dynamics.
3. Pest control — recurring revenue model, larger account count per metro.
4. Garage door / electrical — round out the trades wedge.
5. Med spa / dental / chiro — non-trades vertical to test motion outside the playbook's home category once trades are proven.

Do not open a second vertical until HVAC produces 5 paying logos and a repeatable close motion. Each new vertical gets its own playbook, sample assets, and messaging pillars — but reuses the engine, sales process, pricing structure, and operating cadence below.

## The Offer

The engine ships the same outputs (images + copy + landing snippet) regardless of vertical, so the tier structure below is the standard wrapper. Per-vertical tweaks happen at the *deliverable theme* level (HVAC = emergency / maintenance / seasonal), not at the price-or-package level.

Three tiers. Lead with the middle.

### Starter — $297/mo
- 6 ad images per month (rotating themes: emergency, maintenance, seasonal)
- 1 campaign concept brief per month
- Delivered as a Drive folder + brand-aligned PDF preview

### Campaign — $497/mo *(anchor offer)*
- 10 ad images per month, 3 angles (emergency / maintenance / seasonal upsell)
- Ad copy variants for Google + Meta (headline, primary text, CTA)
- One-page landing-section snippet (HTML/copy block) per campaign
- Monthly refresh call (15 min)

### Authority — $997/mo
- Everything in Campaign tier
- 20 images, 4 campaigns/quarter, A/B copy variants
- UTM-tagged tracking links and a monthly performance check-in
- Priority turnaround (48 hr)

**Pricing principles:**
- No free pilots. Free training pilots devalue the work and do not convert.
- Annual prepay = 2 months free (locks revenue, surfaces real buyers).
- All tiers month-to-month after that — friction-free cancel removes the biggest objection on cold calls.

## Sales Motion

Three-touch outbound, sequenced over 8 business days per prospect.

### Touch 1 — Phone (Day 1)
Use the playbook. Open with the brand-specific value prop already written. Goal: book a 15-min discovery slot, *not* close.

> "Hi [name], this is Theo with ProPlan. I work with Dallas HVAC operators on ad creative — I put together a sample campaign for [brand] specifically around [their angle, e.g., 24/7 emergency response]. Worth 15 minutes to walk you through it Thursday or Friday?"

### Touch 2 — Email with sample (Day 2)
Send 2–3 generated ad images branded for their company, with the campaign theme matching their playbook entry. Subject: `Sample ads for [Brand] — Dallas summer campaign`. Body under 80 words.

### Touch 3 — Follow-up call (Day 8)
Reference the email. If no answer, leave one voicemail naming the sample. Move to "no" after this touch — no extended drip.

### Discovery → Close (15-min call)
- 3 minutes: their current ad activity, what's working/not
- 5 minutes: walk through the sample images and explain the monthly cadence
- 5 minutes: pricing tiers, default to Campaign ($497)
- 2 minutes: take a card or send a Stripe link before the call ends

**Target conversion:** 20 prospects → 6 discovery calls → 2–3 paid logos in cycle 1.

## Channels

### Primary (now)
- **Outbound phone + email** to the 20-prospect HVAC playbook in DFW.
- **Drive-by referrals** — once one shop is happy, ask for one introduction to a non-competing shop in an adjacent ZIP.

### Secondary (after 5 logos)
- **HVAC Facebook groups** (Texas operator-only groups) — share generated samples as proof of work, not pitches.
- **ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro user communities** — same logic, vertical software user bases.
- **Local trade associations** (TACCA-North Texas) — sponsor a session, not a booth.

### Avoid (for now)
- Paid ads (we do not yet have a CAC model worth scaling).
- Content marketing / SEO (too slow for the seasonal window).
- Cold LinkedIn (HVAC owners do not live there).

## Pricing & Packaging Tests

Run these in sequence, not parallel.

| Test | Hypothesis | Decide after |
|---|---|---|
| $497 anchor vs. $297 anchor | $497 with copy + landing converts better than $297 image-only | 10 discovery calls |
| Annual prepay discount | 20%+ of buyers will prepay for 2 months free | 5 closes |
| Per-campaign add-on ($150) | Existing customers will buy extra one-off campaigns | 30 days post-close |
| Setup fee ($497 one-time) | Reduces churn risk by raising commitment | Cycle 2 cohort |

## Operating Cadence

### Weekly (Theo)
- Monday: 10 outbound calls + 10 sample-email sends
- Wednesday: discovery calls (target 3)
- Friday: deliver assets to active customers, batch-render next week's images

### Monthly
- First Monday: review pipeline, kill stalled prospects, refresh playbook with 5 new researched targets
- Mid-month: customer check-ins (Campaign + Authority tiers)
- Last Friday: pricing/offer review against the test table above

### Quarterly
- Vertical expansion review (open roofing/plumbing only when HVAC has 5+ logos)
- Tooling review — what is breaking when we run >10 customers manually?

## Metrics That Matter

Track only these in cycle 1. Everything else is noise.

| Metric | Target by Day 30 | Target by Day 90 |
|---|---|---|
| Outbound dials/week | 50 | 80 |
| Discovery calls booked | 6 | 20 |
| Paid logos | 2 | 6 |
| MRR | $1,000 | $3,500 |
| Logo churn | n/a | <1 in first cohort |
| Avg time per customer/week | <2 hr | <90 min (forces productization) |

When per-customer time stops shrinking, that is the signal to invest in self-serve or a VA layer — not before.

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Customer expects lead-gen results, not just creative | Set expectation in writing during discovery: "We deliver creative; lead volume depends on spend, targeting, and offer." Reinforce in onboarding email. |
| AI-image fatigue / homeowner skepticism | Mix generated imagery with stock-photo composites + brand overlays. Avoid uncanny human faces in v1. |
| Meta / Google ad policy flags on copy | Hard-code copy guardrails in the engine: no "lowest price," "guaranteed," "best in Dallas" claims. |
| Operator (Theo) becomes the bottleneck past 15–20 accounts | Build the asset-delivery pipeline (Drive folder auto-populate + email send) before logo #10. |
| One metro / one vertical concentration | Open vertical #2 (roofing) at logo #5 to de-risk. |

## 30 / 60 / 90 Day Plan

### Days 1–30 — Validate
- Close 2 paid logos from the existing 20-prospect playbook
- Lock in $497 Campaign tier pricing or move to a tested alternative
- Ship copy + landing-snippet deliverable alongside images (close the agency-vs-DIY gap)

### Days 31–60 — Repeat
- 4 more paid logos (total 6)
- Onboard one ServiceTitan-using shop to validate tech-stack ICP
- Document the asset delivery process so it is repeatable, not heroic
- First customer renewal — measure churn signal honestly

### Days 61–90 — Expand
- Open roofing as vertical #2 by building a 20-prospect roofing playbook against the same engine — no product rebuild
- Ship one productization upgrade (auto-deliver to Drive, or self-serve brief intake) that benefits all verticals equally
- Decide: bootstrap path ($30–60K MRR / 4–5 verticals on one engine) vs. partnership/acquihire conversation with a vertical SaaS

## Decision Triggers

Pre-commit to these so the path forks happen on data, not vibes.

- **Kill HVAC and try a different vertical** if cycle 1 produces 0 paid logos from 20 well-worked prospects.
- **Pause new sales and productize** if per-customer weekly time exceeds 3 hours at logo #8.
- **Raise prices** if the first 6 closes happen with zero pricing pushback.
- **Bring on a VA / contractor** if MRR exceeds $5K and Theo's outbound time drops below 5 hrs/week.

## Open Questions

These are unresolved and should be revisited monthly:
- Do customers value the copy + landing snippet, or just the images? (Affects whether to keep Starter at all.)
- Is monthly cadence the right rhythm, or should it be per-campaign?
- Where does the engine break first under multi-tenant load — image quota, brand consistency, or delivery?
- What is the right second vertical: roofing (bigger ticket, similar motion) or plumbing (closer overlap, easier referral)?
